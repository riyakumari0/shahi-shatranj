The Book of the Game

How to play
  • Tap a piece to lift it. Golden dots show where it may go; red rings show captures. Tap a dot to move.
  • Drag to look around the board, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • With Drone on, the camera flies to the side whose turn it is.
  • Keys: U undo · N new · D drone · T top view · V your view · Esc drop piece
The courtiers
♔
King — Padishah. One square any way. Must never stay in check. Can castle.
♕
Queen — Malika. Any distance straight or diagonal.
♖
Rook — Rukh, the Fortress. Any distance straight along rows and columns.
♗
Bishop — Wazir. Any distance diagonally; stays on one colour.
♘
Knight — Aswar, the Cavalier. L-shaped leap; jumps over others.
♙
Pawn — Sipahi. Forward one (two on first move), captures diagonally, en passant, promotes on the last rank.
How a game ends
  • Checkmate wins. Draws: stalemate, threefold repetition, fifty moves without a capture or pawn move, or too little material to mate.
